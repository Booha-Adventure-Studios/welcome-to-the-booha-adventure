Welcome to the Booha Adventure.
This isn’t just a page with games on it. It’s a learning world that opens a little more every time you move forward. I built Booha after watching students study English, forget it, study it again, and slowly start to think the problem was them. It wasn’t. The real problem was that English never stayed in one place long enough to feel familiar. Booha gives English a place to live. Every week, you move through the maze and reach a checkpoint. When you reach it, two things open at the same time: your weekly study decks and your weekly Booha adventure game decks. One warms up your brain. The other lets you use what you warmed up. If you touch both, even a little each day, English stops feeling heavy and starts feeling normal. Less pressure. More momentum. Booha isn’t about rushing or being perfect. It’s about moving forward, one checkpoint at a time.

Important note about your Booha login
Your Booha PIN can only be used on one device at a time. If someone logs in using your name and PIN while you are already logged in, your current session will automatically end. The Booha Adventure is free for students. In the future, additional PINs will be available for 500 yen per month. Please keep your PIN private. If your session ends, just log in again and keep going.
Important:
Outside of taking notes, I do not want students to write in class anymore. Writing happens at home so class time can be used for speaking and real communication.

Weekly diary rule:
Every student should keep a diary. Each week, write the weekly vocabulary in your diary in English and Japanese, and bring it to school for me to check. If you don’t know what to write, use the Boo-riculum’s weekly diary / writing tips.

Adults: You’re an adult… so don’t pay for English and then do nothing.
The goal is to move away from scripts and into real communication. Come prepared. Common habits that block progress include:
・Trying to speak English with Japanese logic
・Wanting “perfect lines” instead of conversations
・Talking at the teacher instead of with the teacher
・Using vague words for everything (enjoy, famous, so-so, busy…)
・Having almost no curiosity about English-speaking culture

Adults improve fastest when they can talk about what they think—not just where they went and what they ate. If preparing is difficult, use the Boo-riculum weekly theme as your topic.
The study decks are the foundation of everything in the Booha Adventure. Nothing else works properly without them. Every week, the study decks always contain vocabulary cards, sentence cards, and question cards, and they are all full audio. The vocabulary cards train your ear and your eyes to recognize the week’s core words quickly. The sentence cards show how those words actually live inside real English, so you don’t memorize isolated pieces. The question cards prepare you to react and answer without freezing, because you’ve already met the language in a calm way before the games ask you to use it. These decks are not meant to be finished once. They are meant to be visited often until the English starts to feel familiar.
The adventure game decks are where the English you studied turns into action. Each weekly adventure deck includes Tap Match, Say the Word, Word Builder, Eigo Pera Pera, Make the Sentences, and Eigo Adventure. Tap Match builds fast recognition. Say the Word builds speaking confidence and accuracy. Word Builder strengthens spelling and word shape. Eigo Pera Pera pushes quick thinking and reaction. Make the Sentences trains structure and word order. Eigo Adventure brings everything together into a weekly challenge that feels like a real moment, not a worksheet. When the study decks feel familiar, these games feel smooth. When the study decks are skipped, these games feel confusing. The system is honest like that.
Booha is designed so your English doesn’t reset every week. In the middle of each month, you’ll see glowing wooden signs appear on the maze. These are numbered markers for the month: January is “1,” February is “2,” and so on. When you reach them, you unlock the monthly review decks. These decks have no audio on purpose. They are there to check what you’ve learned, not teach something new. The review activities are Word Typing, Pick the Sentence, Word Speed, Spot the Mistake, Sentence Order, and Yes / No Check. These games look simple because the controls are simple, but guessing won’t take you far. The only way forward is understanding. And sometimes you’ll see magical trees appear somewhere on the maze. These special trees unlock unique review-style challenges. They are landmarks in the world, showing that your skills have connected enough to unlock something new.
This is the living part of the Booha Adventure. When games are added, systems change, or bugs are fixed, I’ll write it here. If you notice something broken, confusing, or interesting enough to improve, email the school so it doesn’t get lost.

大人： 大人なのに、英語にお金を払って何もしないのはもったいないです。
台本（セリフ）から卒業して、本当の会話に移りましょう。準備して来てください。
よくある伸びない原因：
・日本語のロジックのまま英語を話そうとする
・「完璧なセリフ」を求めすぎる
・先生と「会話」ではなく先生に「話しかけるだけ」になる
・便利なあいまい単語だけで済ませる（enjoy / famous / so-so / busy など）
・英語圏の文化への好奇心が少ない

大人は「どこへ行った／何を食べた」だけじゃなく、「何を考えたか」を話せるようになると一気に伸びます。
何を準備すればいいか迷うときは、週のテーマ（ブーリキュラム）を話題にしてください。
学習デッキは、ブーハーの土台です。ここが弱いと、他がうまく回りません。 毎週、学習デッキには必ず「単語カード」「文カード」「質問カード」の3つが入っています。全部フル音声です。 単語カードは、その週の中心になる単語を、音と文字の両方で素早く認識できるようにします。文カードは、単語が本当の英語の中でどう使われるかを見せます。 質問カードは、ゲームで答えるときに固まらないように、先に落ち着いて英語に触れておくための準備です。 1回で終わらせるものではありません。何度も開いて、英語が「見たことある」「聞いたことある」になっていくのが目的です。
アドベンチャーゲームデッキは、学習した英語を「使える形」に変える場所です。 毎週のデッキには、Tap Match、Say the Word、Word Builder、Eigo Pera Pera、Make the Sentences、Eigo Adventure が入っています。 Tap Matchは認識スピード。Say the Wordは発音と自信。Word Builderはスペリングと単語の形。 Eigo Pera Peraは反応の速さ。Make the Sentencesは語順と文の作り方。Eigo Adventureは全部をまとめた週のチャレンジです。 学習デッキが慣れているほど、ゲームはスムーズになります。学習を飛ばすと、ゲームが急に難しく感じます。ブーハーはそこが正直です。
ブーハーは、毎週リセットされる英語にならないように作っています。 月の真ん中頃になると、迷路のどこかに「光る木の看板」が出てきます。これは月の番号の目印です。1は1月、2は2月、という感じです。 そこに行くと、その月全体の復習デッキが開きます。復習デッキは、音声なしです。わざとです。学んだものが、本当に残っているかをチェックするためです。 復習の活動は、Word Typing、Pick the Sentence、Word Speed、Spot the Mistake、Sentence Order、Yes / No Check です。 操作は簡単に見えますが、適当に押しても前には進めません。理解している人が強いゲームです。 そして、ときどき迷路のどこかに「魔法の木」が現れます。これは特別な復習チャレンジが開く場所です。飾りではなく、世界のランドマークです。
ここはブーハーの「生きている場所」です。ゲームの追加、仕組みの変更、バグの修正などは、ここに書きます。 もし何かおかしい、分かりにくい、もっと良くできそう、と思ったら、学校にメールしてください。埋もれないようにするためです。

This is a world. English is the entry room. You can review and practice all the weekly content without going any further. The study decks, the audio, the games — everything you need for class is right here. But the maze is where the weekly games live. And past the maze is something else entirely.

The further you travel, the wilder and darker it gets. Karasuki is a doorway. Once you're through it, you might get lost. That's not a warning. That's the point.

Play the games. Unlock the forest. Find the doorways. Discover what opens.

Tests appear on scheduled days. When a test day arrives, you'll access it here. Stars earned each week are yours to keep. Progress inside Karasuki resets every Monday — because every week is a different world. Nothing happens the same way twice.

Why I built it I watched students study English, forget it, study it again, and slowly start to think the problem was them. It wasn't. The problem was that English never stayed in one place long enough to feel familiar. Booha gives English a place to live — and a reason to come back.

Adults
  • Come prepared. The goal is to move away from scripts and into real communication.
  • Common habits that block progress: speaking English with Japanese logic, wanting perfect lines instead of conversations, using vague words for everything (enjoy, famous, so-so, busy), having almost no curiosity about English-speaking culture.
  • Adults improve fastest when they talk about what they think — not just where they went and what they ate.
Study Decks The study decks are the foundation of everything. Nothing else works properly without them. Every week includes vocabulary cards, sentence cards, and question cards, all with full audio, along with a hands-free stream for listening and repeating.

The vocabulary cards train your ear and eyes to recognize the week's core words quickly. The sentence cards show how those words live inside real English. The question cards prepare you to respond, not just understand, so when it's time to speak you're not starting from zero.

The hands-free stream is where everything connects. You listen, repeat, and move with the language without stopping. This is where rhythm, confidence, and flow build.

These decks are not something you finish once. You come back to them until the English feels familiar.

Game Decks Each week includes 9 games built around the same English you studied.

The games are not separate from the study. They are where you use it. When the study decks feel familiar, the games feel smooth. When the study decks are skipped, the games feel hard. The system is honest like that.

As you complete games, more things begin to appear and unlock across the Adventure. The more you play, the more the world responds.

スタディデッキ スタディデッキはブーハーの土台です。ここが弱いと、他がうまく回りません。毎週、単語カード・文カード・質問カードに加えて、ハンズフリーで聞いてリピートできるストリームが入っています。すべて音声付きです。

単語カードは、その週の中心となる単語を音と文字の両方で素早く認識できるようにします。文カードは、その単語が実際の英語の中でどう使われるかを見せます。質問カードは、「分かる」ではなく「答える」ための準備です。

ストリームはすべてをつなぐ場所です。止めずに聞いて、繰り返して、英語の流れに乗る。ここでリズムと自信がついていきます。

1回で終わらせるものではありません。英語が「見たことある・聞いたことある」状態になるまで、何度も開いてください。

ゲームデッキ 毎週、同じ英語を使った9つのゲームが用意されています。

ゲームは学習とは別ではありません。ここで英語を使います。スタディデッキに慣れているとスムーズに進み、飛ばすと急に難しく感じます。ブーハーはそこが正直です。

ゲームを進めるほど、アドベンチャーの中でいろいろなものが現れ、解放されていきます。進めば進むほど、世界が反応します。
